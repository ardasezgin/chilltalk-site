ChillTalk Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 13, 2026

In short: ChillTalk turns your speech into text and, only after you tap Allow, sends that text to Google Gemini (a third-party AI service) to reply to you and to prepare your feedback. Your chats are saved so you can review them. There are no ads, no tracking, and we never sell your data. You can delete everything from the app at any time.

Who we are

ChillTalk is an English speaking practice app developed by Arda Sezgin ("we"). Contact: arda@ardasezgin.com.

What we collect

  • An anonymous account ID. The app creates an anonymous account automatically. We do not ask for your name, email or phone number.
  • Your conversations as text. What you say and what the AI partner replies, plus the chat topic and time.
  • Your feedback and words. The feedback cards and the words suggested to you.
  • Daily usage counts. How many replies and feedback cards you used today, to keep the service fair for everyone.

We do not store audio recordings. We do not collect your location, contacts, photos, or advertising identifiers.

How your data is processed

  • Speech recognition (Apple). Your voice is turned into text by Apple's speech recognition. When your iPhone supports it, this happens on the device; otherwise Apple processes the audio under Apple's privacy policy.
  • AI replies and feedback (Google Gemini). The text of your conversation is sent to Google's Gemini API to write the partner's replies and your feedback card. See Third-party AI service below for exactly what is sent.
  • Storage (Supabase). Your account, conversations, feedback and words are stored with Supabase in the European Union (Frankfurt, Germany).

Third-party AI service (Google Gemini)

Chill, the conversation partner, and your feedback cards are generated by Google Gemini, an AI service provided by Google LLC through the Gemini API.

  • Your permission comes first. Before your first conversation, the app shows a screen that explains what is sent and to whom, with Allow and Don't Allow buttons. Nothing is sent to Google unless you tap Allow. You can turn this off at any time in Settings → Share conversations with Google Gemini; the app then asks again before the next conversation.
  • What is sent: the text of what you say in a chat (as transcribed by Apple's speech recognition), the partner's previous replies in that chat, the chat topic, and a few of your saved practice words so the partner can use them naturally.
  • What is never sent: your voice recording, name, email, phone number, contacts, location, photos, account ID or any other data from your device.
  • How it is collected: through the microphone, only while a conversation is open and you are speaking; the speech is converted to text by Apple's speech recognition before anything leaves your device for Gemini.
  • What it is used for: only to generate the partner's replies and your feedback card. The request goes from our server to Google; the app never talks to Google directly.
  • How Google protects it: we use the paid Gemini API. Under the Gemini API Terms, Google does not use prompts or responses to improve its products or train its models, processes them as a data processor under its Data Processing Addendum, and keeps logs only for a limited time to detect abuse. This gives your data the same level of protection as described in this policy.

Why we use it

Only to run the app: to have the conversation with you, to show your feedback, to bring your new words back into future chats, and to apply daily usage limits. We don't use your data for advertising and we don't sell or share it with anyone other than the services listed above.

How long we keep it

Until you delete it. Open Settings → Delete my data in the app to permanently delete your account and everything linked to it. Deleting the app alone does not delete your data from our servers, so please use this option first.

Age requirement

ChillTalk is intended for adults aged 18 and over. It is not directed at anyone under 18, and we do not knowingly collect data from them. If you believe someone under 18 has used the app, contact us and we will delete their data.

Your rights

You can delete your data in the app at any time. For any other request about your data, contact us at arda@ardasezgin.com.

Changes

If this policy changes, we'll update the date above. If what we share with third parties changes, the app will ask for your permission again.
