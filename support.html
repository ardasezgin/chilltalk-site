ChillTalk Support

Just talk. No homework.

How does it work?

Tap Talk, answer Chill's question, and keep chatting. When you stop speaking for a moment, ChillTalk sends what you said and Chill replies. Tap Finish whenever you like to get a short feedback card with up to three tips and a few useful words.

Chill can't hear me

  • Make sure ChillTalk has access to the Microphone and Speech Recognition in iPhone Settings → ChillTalk.
  • Check that your iPhone isn't in silent or low-power recording mode and that no other app is using the microphone.
  • Tap Retry on the chat screen.

Chill's voice sounds robotic

Download a higher-quality English voice: iPhone Settings → Accessibility → Spoken Content → Voices → English, then choose a voice marked Enhanced or Premium. ChillTalk uses the best installed voice automatically.

"Come back tomorrow"

ChillTalk has a generous daily limit to keep the service fair. It resets every day.

Delete my data

Open Settings → Delete my data in the app. See the privacy policy for details.
